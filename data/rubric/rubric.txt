Content (Is the argument developed and supported?)
1 – No clear position or completely off-topic.
2 – A position is stated but reasons or examples are missing or irrelevant.
3 – Position is clear with some supporting reasons; examples may be limited or underdeveloped.
4 – Position is well-supported with relevant reasons and examples; argument is mostly consistent.
5 – Position is clearly argued throughout with strong, relevant reasons and specific examples.

Organization (Is the essay structured and easy to follow?)
1 – No discernible structure; very hard to follow.
2 – Some attempt at structure but ideas are jumbled or transitions are absent.
3 – Basic structure is present; ideas are mostly in order but connections between paragraphs are weak.
4 – Clear structure with logical paragraph order and transitions that help the reader follow the argument.
5 – Well-organized throughout; each paragraph develops a single idea and connects clearly to the overall argument.

Language (Is the writing clear and grammatically accurate?)
1 – Frequent grammatical errors that make the essay difficult to understand.
2 – Several errors that distract from meaning, though the message is recoverable.
3 – Meaning is generally clear; errors are present but do not seriously affect comprehension.
4 – Mostly accurate grammar and vocabulary; errors are minor and infrequent.
5 – Accurate grammar throughout with a varied vocabulary; writing is fluent and easy to read.
